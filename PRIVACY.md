# Pulse AI Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Pulse AI (@Pulseaihealthbot)** handles information when you use the bot through Telegram.

By using Pulse AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About Pulse AI

Pulse AI is a bilingual artificial-intelligence health information assistant providing general health information in English and Arabic.

Pulse AI is **not a doctor, healthcare provider, hospital, emergency service, or substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, examination, or treatment**.

Pulse AI does not provide medical diagnoses and should not be relied upon to make emergency or critical healthcare decisions.

If you believe you or another person may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare professional immediately.

## 2. Information We May Process

When you communicate with Pulse AI, information may include information made available through Telegram and information that you voluntarily send to the bot, such as:

- Telegram user ID
- Telegram username or basic profile information where available
- Messages and questions
- Symptoms or health information you choose to provide
- Language preferences
- Technical information reasonably necessary to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and maintain the service

Because Pulse AI provides health-related information, users may voluntarily submit information that could be considered sensitive.

Please provide only the information reasonably necessary for your question.

## 3. Sensitive Information

Please do not send unnecessary confidential or identifying information, including passwords, authentication codes, payment-card information, bank information, passport or national identification numbers, exact home addresses, or other people's private information without their permission.

Users are responsible for deciding what information they choose to provide to Pulse AI.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- Understand and respond to user questions
- Generate general health information
- Provide general information about symptoms
- Suggest reasonable next steps
- Identify situations where urgent professional medical attention may be appropriate
- Provide responses in English or Arabic
- Operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot Pulse AI
- Prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents
- Comply with applicable legal requirements

Pulse AI does not make automated medical diagnoses.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Pulse AI uses artificial-intelligence technology to generate responses.

Information submitted to the bot may be transmitted to an artificial-intelligence service provider, including **OpenAI**, for processing and generation of responses.

AI-generated information can contain mistakes, omissions, or outdated information. Important medical information should be verified with a qualified healthcare professional.

Third-party AI providers process information according to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and data-processing practices.

## 6. Telegram

Pulse AI operates through Telegram.

Telegram may process information related to your use of Telegram and the bot according to Telegram's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Pulse AI does not control Telegram's independent data-processing, storage, or security practices.

## 7. Hosting and Service Providers

Pulse AI may rely on third-party technology providers required to operate the service, including:

- Telegram
- OpenAI or other artificial-intelligence providers
- Cloud hosting providers, including infrastructure used to run the bot
- Security and technical service providers

Information may be processed by these providers where reasonably necessary to operate Pulse AI.

## 8. Data Retention

Pulse AI aims to minimize unnecessary storage of personal information.

Information may be temporarily processed or retained when reasonably necessary to generate responses, maintain service functionality, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse or security incidents, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Information will not intentionally be retained longer than reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Third-party providers may maintain information according to their own applicable retention policies.

## 9. Data Sharing and Sale

Pulse AI does **not sell users' personal information**.

Pulse AI does **not rent users' personal information to advertisers**.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the service, process user requests, protect the service, comply with applicable law, or respond to valid legal requests.

## 10. Health Information and Medical Disclaimer

Any symptoms or health information submitted to Pulse AI are provided voluntarily by the user.

Pulse AI provides **general informational and educational content only**.

Pulse AI does not establish a doctor-patient relationship.

Pulse AI does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, replace medical examinations, or replace the advice of a licensed healthcare professional.

Do not delay obtaining professional medical care because of information received from Pulse AI.

For serious, worsening, unusual, or potentially life-threatening symptoms, seek appropriate professional medical assistance.

## 11. Children

Pulse AI is not intended to replace professional pediatric medical care.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for a minor to use an online service or provide personal information, Pulse AI should only be used with the appropriate consent and supervision.

Medical concerns involving children should be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional when necessary.

## 12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to help protect information processed through Pulse AI.

However, no online service, messaging platform, cloud provider, or artificial-intelligence system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary confidential, sensitive, or identifying information.
